# Public Portfolio Home Contract V1

## Purpose

The public OVERVA experience has two explicit navigation levels:

1. **OVERVA Home** is a browser-local portfolio of separate workspaces and a
   safe place to start a structured digital-need request from a description,
   reference pattern, file, image, or current system context.
2. **Workspace Studio** is one selected workspace's governed conversation,
   preview, memory, and seven-stage delivery journey.

The portfolio is not an operating automated marketplace and the workspace is
not a global dashboard. A clearly labelled reference-request board may teach a
first-time customer how to describe a need, but it must not present samples as
real demand, bids, ratings, or completed delivery. Moving between Home and a
workspace must preserve workspace identity and must never merge evidence from
separate jobs.

## Home responsibilities

Home may show:

- create-new-need entry;
- search over browser-local workspace names and product descriptions;
- recent workspace cards;
- current delivery stage and next stage for each saved checkpoint;
- entry paths for description, file/data, image/diagram, and current systems;
- clearly labelled reference request patterns for the bounded initial wedge;
- a browser-local structured request draft covering work type, business area,
  capabilities, problem, desired outcome, budget context, period, and intended
  visibility;
- an optional OVERVA completeness review which remains separate from human
  confirmation;
- a deliberate link to the authenticated tenant application.

The public promise is deliberately narrower than the long-term ecosystem:

- begin from one real organizational problem, not a software category;
- return a confirmed requirement, first preview, and an understandable next step;
- let delivery options appear only after the need is sufficiently clear;
- describe the emerging developer network as a concierge pilot, not as an
  already-operating automated marketplace.

Home does not provision a tenant, upload a file, accept a commercial agreement,
deploy a product, or claim that preview activity is production execution.

## Workspace responsibilities

Opening a card selects one stable workspace ID, then loads only that workspace's
checkpoint. The workspace keeps the existing Understand -> Build -> Team Preview
-> Scope -> Commercial Agreement -> Deployment -> Live Operation gates.

Starting from a Home prompt or reference pattern first opens the structured
request form. Submitting that form stores an unpublished browser-local draft,
opens a separate workspace, and adds the standardized request package to that
workspace for human review. A file chooser remains browser-local under the
existing material boundary.

Each browser-local request draft has its own stable draft ID, revision, and
source workspace ID. The workspace checkpoint carries the matching draft ID.
Creating another request must append another draft instead of overwriting the
earlier request. Legacy single-draft storage migrates non-destructively on the
next request save. These identifiers provide local traceability only; they are
not authenticated identity, server-side publication, or Market authorization.

## Capability navigation

Connector marketplace, academy, community, and broad developer-ecosystem claims
do not belong in the standard customer's seven-stage sidebar. A real systems or
integration need enters through Home and is evaluated inside the workspace's
Systems preview and Scope stage. Future contributor surfaces require separate
evidence, authorization, and product validation.

## V17 positioning boundary

V17 changes public acquisition language and information hierarchy only. It
does not rename or reorder the internal seven delivery gates, implement the V18
structured requirement artifact, create the V19 procurement package, or claim
that ranking, bidding, escrow, and automated developer matching are available.

The primary Home question is:

> Байгууллагынхаа ямар ажлыг цахимжуулах хэрэгтэй байна?

Every visible outcome under that question must help a first-time organization
understand what it receives before any software category, connector, developer,
or commercial path is introduced.

## V18 request-intake slice

The first V18 public slice replaces mandatory chat-first entry with a structured
request form and an educational reference-request board. It preserves the V17
primary question and the existing governed Workspace Studio, but makes AI or
advisor review optional from the customer's perspective.

This slice is a request draft and workspace handoff only. It does not publish a
request, expose it to developers, collect proposals, rank vendors, accept money,
form a contract, or claim a production outcome. Those remain separate human and
future server-side gates under the market-entry strategy.

## V20 Home material intake

Selecting `Файл, өгөгдөл` or `Зураг, схем` on Home is part of request intake,
not permission to create a workspace immediately. Selection keeps Home active,
opens the structured request form, and visibly identifies the pending material.
Only form submission creates the unpublished request draft and its linked
workspace.

The public material boundary remains browser-local. Request-draft and workspace
memory may retain the material name, media type, size, and kind as evidence
metadata. They must not place file content, object URLs, extracted customer
records, or a false AI-analysis claim in local storage. The independent
Workspace Studio material action remains available after a workspace exists.

## V21 market-board information hierarchy

Home may use the proven information hierarchy of a request marketplace without
claiming that an operating automated marketplace exists. The first screen leads
with one request list and one create-request action. Its standard navigation is:

1. `Бүх хүсэлт` — clearly labelled reference requests during the concierge
   pilot, with search and bounded business categories;
2. `Миний хүсэлт` — unpublished browser-local request drafts;
3. `Миний ажил` — saved browser-local workspace checkpoints;
4. `Дүрэм` — request quality, evidence separation, human gates, prohibited
   work, and current capability boundaries.

Reference requests must be labelled `ЖИШИГ` and `Бодит захиалга биш`. Disabled
status tabs may explain future lifecycle states, but must not imply real counts.
The Rules view must state that developer proposals, escrow/payment, automatic
contracts, arbitration, and verified marketplace reviews are not current
production capabilities. AI conversation remains optional and appears only
after the person chooses to create and refine a request; it is not a mandatory
landing-page gate.

## V22 customer and provider work contexts

The public Market shell must not use one ambiguous `My Work` concept for two
different sides. A visible work-context switch separates customer requests and
projects from provider opportunities, proposals, delivery jobs, and execution
rules.

One future account may use both contexts, but records and permissions remain
role-attributable. Customer projects are request-derived Workspace checkpoints;
provider jobs are selected Market engagements. Until server-backed provider
identity and proposals exist, provider proposal/job views show an explicit
empty concierge-pilot state and sample request actions cannot pretend to apply
for real work.

For legacy V24 behavior, the delivery explanation follows the still-valid
privacy and bilateral-acceptance parts of D-020: selected scope, milestones, delivery
evidence, customer acceptance or change, production verification, commercial
reconciliation, bilateral review, and closure remain available when parties
choose the governed delivery record. For V26 and later Market work, D-023
supersedes any implication that this full record or a Platform workspace is
mandatory for every product purchase or freelance delivery.

## V26 public market areas

The public Home primary navigation is:

1. `Маркет` — the default arrival area for comparing ready products;
2. `Форум` — community questions, answers, and implementation experience;
3. `Захиалгат ажил` — customer orders, provider proposals, and delivery context.

`Захиалгат ажил` is the preferred Mongolian product label; `Freelance` is not a
primary navigation label. Customer/provider role switching belongs only to this
area and must not make Market appear to be an OVERVA Apps sales department.

OVERVA Apps may appear in Market only as a clearly identified supplier, using
the same product-card structure and future eligibility rules as other
suppliers. Operator controls, ranking, moderation, enforcement, and
competitor-private data are not supplier capabilities.

Until server-backed capabilities exist, product cards and forum topics must be
labelled as samples. Prices, purchase actions, product submission, posting,
accounts, supplier onboarding, reviews, and real order counts must remain
disabled or absent. A preview shell is not evidence of an operating market or
community.

## V27 Group-aligned terminology

The third public area is named `Захиалгат ажил ба үйлчилгээ`. Its future scope
includes work orders, developers, implementation partners, consulting, support,
and maintenance; the current browser-local request board does not claim those
directories or services are live.

Product Market uses the categories `Аппууд`, `Модулиуд`, `Холбогчид`,
`Загварууд`, and `AI агентууд`. `Маркетын засаглал` may be explained as an
operator responsibility, but it is not a fourth customer marketplace and must
not expose operator controls to suppliers. Until real policies and backend
controls exist, its action remains disabled and its wording must not imply
certification or dispute handling is operational.
