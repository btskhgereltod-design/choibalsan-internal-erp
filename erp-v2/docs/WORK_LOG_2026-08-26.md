# OVERVA Work Log — 2026-08-26

## End-of-day checkpoint

### Stable product center

OVERVA helps people understand a business and its data, turn that understanding
into a useful application, test it safely, and operate an approved version. The
implementation may serve beginners, business owners, analysts, professional
developers, implementation partners, and app-service companies at different
levels of complexity without showing every user the same tools.

### Evidence learned today

- A large or already-operating business owner is unlikely to spend substantial
  time configuring a complex ERP personally. Owners are more likely to describe,
  review, correct, approve, buy, and use a result.
- Professional developers, business/data analysts, internal IT teams,
  implementation consultants, agencies, students, and serious app enthusiasts
  may be the frequent Studio users.
- Self-build remains possible, especially for a focused small application, but
  it should not be assumed to be every customer's preferred behavior.
- A creator/service ecosystem may emerge if OVERVA makes business understanding,
  data modeling, preview, testing, deployment, and operation easier.
- Choibalsan camera, lighting, Smart Import, and ERP workflows provide real
  evidence and potential reference applications; they remain tenant examples,
  not universal definitions.

### Active product hypothesis

Use three related surfaces:

1. **OVERVA Studio** — creators analyze, model, build, test, version, and deploy.
2. **Client Review** — business stakeholders inspect, correct, and approve in a
   deliberately simple experience.
3. **OVERVA Runtime** — approved applications operate on OVERVA Cloud, an agreed
   private environment, or another governed delivery target.

This direction is promising but is not yet a fully accepted architecture or
market decision. It must be tested with actual creators and business reviewers.

### Do not forget

- The public advisor remains deterministic/browser-local; it must not be
  represented as provider-backed semantic AI.
- A generic preview is not proof that an app was generated from customer facts.
- Do not reconnect tenant provisioning before scope, commercial, deployment,
  and operations records exist behind the gates.
- Do not replace the live legacy Choibalsan system without parity, backup,
  migration, rollback, authorization, and human UAT evidence.
